Emergency medical situations require immediate attention. Signs of a medical emergency include: chest pain or pressure, difficulty breathing, sudden severe headache, sudden weakness or numbness on one side of the body, difficulty speaking, confusion, severe bleeding, head injury with loss of consciousness, seizures, severe allergic reaction (anaphylaxis) with difficulty breathing or swelling of the face/throat, and poisoning. In case of emergency, call local emergency services immediately (911 in US, 108 in India). Do not drive yourself to the hospital if you are experiencing a medical emergency. If someone is unconscious, check for breathing and pulse, and begin CPR if trained. Keep the person warm and comfortable until help arrives.
