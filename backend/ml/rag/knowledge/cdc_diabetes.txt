Diabetes affects approximately 37 million Americans, with 90-95% having type 2 diabetes. According to the CDC, 1 in 5 people with diabetes don't know they have it. Prediabetes affects 96 million US adults. Type 2 diabetes risk increases with age, especially after 45, and is strongly linked to overweight, physical inactivity, and family history. Symptoms include increased thirst, frequent urination, extreme hunger, unexplained weight loss, fatigue, blurred vision, and slow-healing sores. The CDC-led National Diabetes Prevention Program (NDPP) has been shown to reduce the risk of developing type 2 diabetes by 58% (71% for those over 60) through lifestyle changes. Prevention includes losing 5-7% of body weight, 150 minutes of moderate physical activity per week, and a healthy eating plan. The ABCs of diabetes management are: A1C below 7%, Blood pressure below 140/90, Cholesterol management, and Smoking cessation. Source: US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) Diabetes Guidelines.
