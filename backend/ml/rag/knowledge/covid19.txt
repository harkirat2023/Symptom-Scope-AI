COVID-19 is a respiratory illness caused by the SARS-CoV-2 coronavirus. Common symptoms include fever, dry cough, fatigue, loss of taste or smell, headache, and shortness of breath. Transmission occurs through respiratory droplets and aerosols. Most cases are mild to moderate, but severe complications including pneumonia, acute respiratory distress syndrome (ARDS), and long COVID can occur. Prevention includes vaccination, mask-wearing, and hand hygiene. Treatment depends on severity and may include supportive care, antiviral medications (Paxlovid), and corticosteroids for severe cases. Emergency warning signs include trouble breathing, persistent chest pain, confusion, inability to wake or stay awake, and bluish lips or face.
