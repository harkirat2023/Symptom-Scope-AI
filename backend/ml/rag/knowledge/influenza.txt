Influenza (flu) is a contagious respiratory illness caused by influenza viruses A and B. Symptoms include sudden onset of fever, cough, sore throat, body aches, fatigue, and headache. Unlike the common cold, flu symptoms are more severe and come on abruptly. Annual vaccination is the best prevention. Antiviral medications (oseltamivir) can reduce duration if started within 48 hours of symptom onset. Most people recover within 1-2 weeks, but complications like pneumonia can occur, especially in high-risk groups (elderly, pregnant, immunocompromised). Seek emergency care for difficulty breathing, chest pain, confusion, or persistent high fever.
