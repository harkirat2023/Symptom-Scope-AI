The common cold is a viral infection of the upper respiratory tract, primarily caused by rhinoviruses. Symptoms include runny nose, sneezing, sore throat, cough, and fatigue. Colds are self-limiting and typically resolve within 7-10 days. Treatment focuses on symptom relief: rest, hydration, over-the-counter cold medications, and saline nasal sprays. Antibiotics are ineffective against viral colds. Medical attention is recommended if fever exceeds 101°F for more than 3 days, or if symptoms worsen significantly.
