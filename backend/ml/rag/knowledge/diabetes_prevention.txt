Type 2 diabetes is a chronic condition affecting how the body metabolizes sugar. Risk factors include overweight, physical inactivity, family history, and age over 45. Prevention strategies include maintaining a healthy weight, regular physical activity (at least 150 minutes per week), a balanced diet rich in whole grains, fruits, vegetables, and lean proteins, limiting sugary beverages and processed foods, and regular blood sugar screening. Early symptoms may include increased thirst, frequent urination, unexplained weight loss, fatigue, and blurred vision. If diagnosed, management includes medication (metformin is first-line), blood glucose monitoring, dietary changes, and regular exercise. Untreated diabetes can lead to complications including heart disease, kidney damage, nerve damage, and vision loss.
