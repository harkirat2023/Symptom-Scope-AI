A heart attack (myocardial infarction) occurs when blood flow to a part of the heart is blocked. Common symptoms include chest pain or discomfort (pressure, squeezing, fullness), pain radiating to the left arm, jaw, or back, shortness of breath, cold sweat, nausea, and lightheadedness. Women may experience atypical symptoms like extreme fatigue, indigestion, and back pain. Risk factors include smoking, high blood pressure, high cholesterol, diabetes, obesity, and family history. Immediate action: call emergency services, chew aspirin (325 mg) if not allergic, and rest in a comfortable position. Do not drive yourself. Early treatment with angioplasty or clot-dissolving drugs improves survival significantly.
